Learning to distinguish forest cover types.

This project addresses forest cover type classification. It is included as computational preparation alongside my research interests in human-centered AI and NLP.

I used a stratified 20,000-record Covertype sample with 54 features and seven classes. The dataset citation, version, and sampling seed will be added with the report.

Attribution matters.

I benchmarked classifiers, fitted training-only preprocessing, and evaluated performance. I compared SGD, Adam-family optimizers, and randomized final-layer optimization under controlled compute budgets, examining learning rate, momentum, and regularization. I also evaluated K-Means and Gaussian Mixture Models alongside PCA, ICA, and randomized projections, measuring cluster quality, label alignment, and downstream neural-network performance.

Evidence before numbers.

Experiments report an increase in Adam test Macro-F1 from 0.6193 to 0.6710 with label smoothing, an absolute increase of 0.0517.

The comparison supports the importance of regularization in this experiment.
